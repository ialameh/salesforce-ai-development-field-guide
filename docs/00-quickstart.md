# 00. Quickstart

Two chapters get you from zero to a working, safe AI-assisted Salesforce development setup in 30 minutes.

The first chapter is a safety checklist. It takes 15 minutes and prevents the five destruction scenarios. The second chapter is the tool setup. It takes another 15 minutes and configures your AI tool to work safely with Salesforce.

If you already have Claude Code or another AI tool configured for Salesforce, run through Q1 as a sanity check and move to Chapter F1.
